Quality Assurance Framework

Our 5-Stage Vetting Pipeline

Every candidate undergoes a zero-compromise vetting protocol prior to quota presentation.

01

Passport & NIN Audit

Biometric authentication and verification of international passports with 24+ months validity.

02

Police Character (PCC)

Criminal record vetting and certified clearances issued by CID police authorities.

03

Health Screening

Rigorous preliminary medical examinations complying with GAMCA and European fitness regulations.

04

English & Skills Test

Evaluation of fluent spoken English, attitude, and job-specific practical competencies.

05

Cultural Briefing

Pre-departure orientation on workplace safety, host-country labor law, and contractual fidelity.
